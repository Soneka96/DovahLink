# 03.10 — Connection Cards / Handoff

**Status:** Paused / requires re-planning

## Outcome

The original goal was to finish the prototype connection surface and hand off correctly from Host
selection through connection and pairing into the connected product/session experience. This step is
not claimed complete.

## Why it exists

Connection cards and post-pair navigation must present current domain state and carry the selected
Host through the real connection flow.

## Scope

The original product intent was for cards to reflect truthful states such as available, known,
connected, unavailable/offline, reconnecting, or repair/re-pair required, and for selection to hand
off into the connected session after the actual pairing/connection result.

## Non-goals

- Do not define new trust or security semantics in the card or navigation layer.
- Do not claim these exact states or transitions form a current SDK state machine.

## Dependencies / authority

Cards consume current SDK/domain lifecycle and the approved security semantics described in the
[identity and transport security architecture](../../../../ai/context/security/identity-and-transport.md).
Prototype layout remains subject to the current
[Flutter architecture conventions](../../../../ai/context/flutter/architecture.md).

## Acceptance criteria

**Original intent, not a current implementation contract:** cards and handoff reflect authoritative
Host, connection, and pairing outcomes without fabricating trust or success.

## History

audit.

## Current disposition
